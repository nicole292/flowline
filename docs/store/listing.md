## Google Play: main store listing

**App name** (max 30): FLOWLINE: Highway Traffic Racer

**Short description** (max 80):
Weave through highway traffic, pass close and build Flow for double points.

**Full description** (max 4000):

Thread your car through rush-hour traffic at 250 km/h. Every close pass fills
your Flow meter. Fill it and Flow Drive doubles your points, until you bump
into something.

HOW IT PLAYS
• Near misses, overtakes and streaks score points. The closer, the more.
• Fill the Flow meter for Flow Drive: double points until your next bump.
• Bumps dent your car instead of ending the run. Keep driving, keep scoring.
• Watch for crash sites ahead: police, cones and smoke close a lane.

THREE WAYS TO DRIVE
• Endless: how far and how fast can you go?
• Two-way: oncoming traffic in the left lanes. Pass on the wrong side for big points.
• Time attack: race the clock from checkpoint to checkpoint.

YOUR GARAGE
• Cars and motorbikes to unlock, from a nimble starter to a supercar.
• Upgrade engine, brakes and handling.
• Paint, rims and your own number plate.

MADE FOR PHONES
• Steering wheel, arrows or gyro (tilt) steering, gas and brake pedals or auto gas.
• Chase, top and cockpit cameras, with your hands on the wheel.
• Day, sunset and night highways.
• Missions, daily rewards and levels.

No ads. No accounts. No data collected. Plays offline.

## App content answers (same approach as Bubblop)
- **Privacy policy**: URL above.
- **Ads**: No, the app contains no ads.
- **App access**: All functionality is available without special access.
- **Content rating (IARC questionnaire)**: Category "Game". Violence: none involving
  people or animals; vehicles crash and dent with no injuries or blood, so answer
  "No" to violence questions unless asked specifically about vehicle damage. No
  fear, sex, language, drugs, gambling, user interaction, sharing or location. Expect
  PEGI 3 / Everyone.
- **Target audience**: 13+ (avoids the Families policy requirements; the game itself is fine for all ages).
- **Data safety**: No data collected, no data shared. Data is not encrypted in transit
  because nothing is transmitted. Users cannot request deletion because nothing is stored off the device.
- **Advertising ID**: No.
- **Government app / financial features / health**: No.
