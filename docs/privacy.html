FLOWLINE privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

What the game collects

Nothing. FLOWLINE does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use the internet.

What stays on your phone

Your best score, distance, in-game cash and settings are saved only on your own device so the game remembers them. They never leave the phone. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Permissions

The game asks for vibration only, used for short haptic feedback during play.

Children

Because the game collects no data, it collects no data from children either.

Changes

If a future version adds something that affects privacy (for example optional ads), this page will be updated first and the change will be described here.
